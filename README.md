### Research Focus

My work bridges **energy engineering** and **applied artificial intelligence** to accelerate reliable, data-driven renewable energy integration:

- **Physics-Informed Digital Twins** for pre-deployment validation of AI forecasting models
- **Wind & Solar (PV) Power Forecasting** using deep learning (LSTM) and semi-analytic predictability benchmarks
- **Smart Energy Systems** & risk management for sustainable energy transitions
- **Applied Computer Vision & Data Fusion** for wildlife/bird monitoring around wind farms and counter-UAV systems

I am committed to **Open and Reproducible Science** — releasing open-source benchmarks, code, and datasets alongside peer-reviewed publications.
